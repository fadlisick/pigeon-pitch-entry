make.fun entry for **Pigeon Pitch, One Line**: one line, at most 200 characters, an original elevator pitch a city pigeon gives to investors for its startup.

`main.py` deterministically writes the locked pitch to `/output/entry.txt`:

> We are Uber, but for breadcrumbs: one tap and three hundred pigeons arrive before your sandwich hits the pavement.
